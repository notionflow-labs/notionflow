BEFORE YOU SEND IT TO NOTION

Check the draft
against your notes.

01

Start with the source

Paste notes, upload TXT, Markdown or PDF, or import an accessible page from the demo Notion workspace.

02

Compare each item with the source

Claude organizes the content into a summary, decisions, tasks and open questions. Task and decision excerpts let you compare the draft with the original.

03

Edit, then export

Correct the wording, owners and dates. Download Markdown or JSON, or save the reviewed draft in our demo Notion workspace.
